⚠️ Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) — Active Protocol
STAGE II IN FORCE

Under GRAP Stage II (Air Quality Index 201–300), the Brihanmumbai Municipal Corporation enforces mandatory dust suppression across all active construction sites and bans open biomass combustion.

  • Daily mechanical / vacuum sweeping of heavy arterial traffic roads.
  • Water sprinkling across ready-mix concrete (RMC) plants and Metro rail corridors.
  • Ban on diesel generator sets except for emergency hospital services.
  • Strict vigilance against open waste combustion at Deonar & Mulund dumping grounds.
